# SPC

**Summary**: Smart point clouds — point cloud data models that carry semantics, topology, provenance and queryable structure so downstream processes can reason over them instead of rebuilding from scratch.
**Last updated**: 10-30-2026.

---

- *3D Data Science with Python* (Poux, O'Reilly 2025): Book-length workflow for building accurate digital environments from point clouds — acquisition with COLMAP/PyCOLMAP or RealityScan, noise removal, subsampling and feature extraction, mesh/voxel/k-d tree/octree modelling, clustering and semantic segmentation, then Open3D/PyVista visualisation. Notes cover why 3D CNNs struggle with point clouds and how PointNet answers that, voxels as the 3D equivalent of pixels, accuracy/precision/recall definitions, and Postshot, CloudCompare and the City of Vancouver 2022 LiDAR dataset as practice material. Related: [[Geospatial]]. Keywords: point cloud, 3D data science, semantic segmentation, PointNet, COLMAP, Open3D, PyVista, voxel, LiDAR, Poux
- *Smart Point Clouds*: Working definition after Laurent Poux (Mapscaping podcast) — every point knows where it is, what it is, what it relates to, and how a downstream process can use it without rebuilding everything from scratch. The note traces the term to Poux, Neuville, Hallot & Billen (ISPRS Annals IV-2/W1, 2016), whose first framework combined device expertise, analytic expertise and domain knowledge, and collects an O'Reilly overview on semantics, topology, 3D Gaussian splatting and spatial AI. Related: [[Geospatial]]. Keywords: smart point cloud, Poux, semantics, topology, spatial AI, Gaussian splatting, provenance, knowledge representation
- [Toward a Unified Smart Point Cloud Framework](https://drive.google.com/file/d/14RXEzupZAjH3qg6OoS_ooX8cOHX4ChC8/view): Salaheldin, Shaker & Li, *Buildings* 16(2):293 (2026) — PRISMA systematic review of 58 SPC studies (22 direct, 22 indirect, 14 new-use) that formalises SPC as queryable, ontology-linked and provenance-aware. Sets out the required elements (point/region/object semantics, relations, multi-LoD, ontology hooks, auditable updates) and distils a configurable eight-module pipeline over the S1–S6 acquisition-to-modelling stages. Hybrid neuro-symbolic classification improves long-tail consistency; open problems remain in scale, domain transfer, 4D updates, surface exports, query usability and sensor fusion. Related: [[Geospatial]]. Keywords: smart point cloud, systematic review, PRISMA, ontology, OWL, SPARQL, semantic segmentation, provenance, scan-to-BIM, 4D
