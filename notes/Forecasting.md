# Forecasting

**Summary**: Landscape conservation forecasting — state-and-transition simulation models and simulation-based evaluation of restoration and conservation innovations.
**Last updated**: 10-30-2026.

---

- [State-and-transition simulation models: a framework for forecasting landscape change](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/2041-210X.12597): Daniel, Frid, Sleeter & Fortin in *Methods in Ecology and Evolution* 7(11):1413–1423 (2016) define the STSM: a landscape of discrete cells advanced as a discrete-time, time-inhomogeneous stochastic process, with a separate transition matrix per transition type rather than one Markov matrix. Adds integer counters (age, time-since-transition), transitions specified as target areas instead of probabilities, and spatial/temporal multipliers for autocorrelated disturbance. ST-Sim on SyncroSim implements it and accepts external R/Python submodels; a Hawai'i LULC case projects agriculture, urbanization, wildfire, shrub encroachment, plantation harvest and climate-driven moisture shifts over 50 years. Related: [[Forecasting]], [[Fire]], [[Conservation]], [[Forest]]. Keywords: STSM, state-and-transition, ST-Sim, SyncroSim, Markov chain, landscape simulation, land-use change, wildfire, Monte Carlo, Hawai'i
- [Forecasting for intended consequences](https://conbio.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/csp2.370): Mozelewski & Scheller in *Conservation Science and Practice* argue that risk-averse, status-quo-biased management overweights unintended consequences and never prices the cost of not innovating. Simulation forecasts let managers test restoration and conservation innovations in silico — comparing intended outcomes, trade-offs, timing and cost against business-as-usual before deployment. Case studies cover passive tropical-dry-forest restoration, fisher fuel-treatment trade-offs, blight-resistant chestnut reintroduction and assisted migration; emerging uses include rewilding and de-extinction. Related: [[Forecasting]], [[Conservation]], [[Fire]], [[Forest]]. Keywords: conservation forecasting, simulation models, status quo bias, restoration, structured decision making, assisted migration, de-extinction, rewilding, trade-offs
- [Conservation International — CPLUS QGIS plugin](https://conservationinternational.github.io/cplus-plugin/): Climate Positive Land Use Strategy (CPLUS) decision-support tool as a QGIS plugin for spatially-explicit land-use planning using natural climate solutions (carbon stocks, sequestration potential) to align national commitments with global climate targets. Model combines NCS pathways, scenario generation, financial (NPV) and carbon accounting with map-repeat models. Piloted in Bushbuckridge, South Africa. Related: [[Geospatial]], [[Software]], [[Forest]]. Keywords: CPLUS, QGIS plugin, land-use planning, natural climate solutions, carbon sequestration, decision support, regeneration scenario modeling
- [Annotated Bibliography: Landscape Conservation Forecasting](Conservation%20Forecasting.md): 11-source annotated bibliography covering Landscape Conservation Forecasting (LCF) methodology — STSMs, species distribution models, ecological departure metrics, habitat suitability, and socio-ecological extensions. Key themes: methodological foundations (Daniel et al. 2016, Blankenship et al. 2015), applied forecasting across Nevada/Utah (Provencher et al. 2011, 2021, 2026), SDM integration (Lawler et al. 2011), and agent-based model coupling (Miller & Frid 2021). Related: [[Fire]], [[Geospatial]]. Keywords: STSM, conservation planning, ecological forecasting, habitat suitability, SyncroSim, nature markets, climate adaptation
