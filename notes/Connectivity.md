# Connectivity

**Summary**: Multi-species landscape connectivity — frameworks and reproducible workflows that turn habitat, trait and movement data into corridor and protected-area network maps.
**Last updated**: 10-30-2026.

---

- [Movement, Water, Connection](https://zenodo.org/records/22979020): Angulo Lopez (Zenodo preprint, 09-26) — a pure-Python circuit-theory connectivity workflow for the Greater Côa Valley, Portugal, built so small rewilding groups without a Julia runtime or clean occurrence data can still run one. Ten focal species grouped by movement medium (land, water, air), Random Forest suitability from GBIF records turned into 100 m resistance surfaces over 10,230 km², current flow solved with a `scipy.sparse` moving-window solver. Adds three locally derived penalties: recency-weighted MODIS fire (10.07% of cells burned 2015–Jul 2025), distance-decayed barriers tiered by observed permeability from 22 field records, and UNESCO heritage areas. The strongest combined corridor runs down the lower Côa to the Douro, and the authors are explicit that 0.78–0.99 training accuracy is not validation. Related: [[Geospatial]], [[Fire]], [[Conservation]]. Keywords: landscape connectivity, circuit theory, Omniscape, resistance surfaces, Random Forest, GBIF, rewilding, Côa Valley, barriers, reproducible workflow
- [A comprehensive framework to assess multi-species landscape connectivity](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/2041-210X.14444): Prima et al. in *Methods in Ecology and Evolution* replace arbitrary surrogate species with data-driven groups — 193 French vertebrates (52 mammals, 141 birds) clustered on environmental niche plus eight connectivity traits into 11 mammal and 19 bird groups. For each group they run Omniscape circuit theory to map ecological continuities, then derive multi-scale network metrics for 1,619 protected areas, propagating uncertainty through the modelling choices. Protection of continuities and PA connectivity both come out low, and different protection types contribute unequally to group-specific habitat connectivity. Related: [[Geospatial]], [[Conservation]]. Keywords: multi-species connectivity, circuit theory, network theory, Omniscape, protected areas, ecological continuities, uncertainty, France, vertebrates
