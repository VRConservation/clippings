# Geospatial

**Summary**: GIS analysis, GDAL/OGR tools, and ArcGIS Pro workflows for geospatial data processing and climate resilience mapping.
**Last updated**: 09-03-2026.

---

- [A1 Collision Density, 2000–2024](https://visquill.com/gallery/uk-a1/): Interactive visualization mapping 25 years of reported road-traffic collisions along the A1 (London–Edinburgh) from UK DfT STATS19 data — collisions projected onto a reference polyline and aggregated per segment into a density histogram, built with VisQuill and MapLibre GL JS. Related: [[Geospatial]]. Keywords: collision density, road safety, STATS19, UK, A1, MapLibre, data visualization
- [Global WPS LULC Static Data: ESA WorldCover 2021 (USGS 24-category, 3 arc-sec)](https://zenodo.org/records/22674626): Updated 2021 global land-use field for the WRF model — ESA WorldCover 2021 v200 cross-mapped by majority vote to the standard USGS 24-category scheme at ~90 m, replacing default 1992-era data; enables genuine fractional land use and improves Noah-MP surface physics (1.5 GB download, includes GEOGRID.TBL). Related: [[Geospatial]], [[Remote_Sensing]]. Keywords: WRF, land use, ESA WorldCover, LULC, Noah-MP, geogrid, 3 arc-second, USGS classification
- [Mastering GDAL Tools (Full Course)](https://courses.spatialthoughts.com/gdal-tools.html): Comprehensive course on GDAL/OGR command-line utilities covering raster merging, format conversion, compression, COG creation, DEM hillshade, satellite band composites, NDVI, pan-sharpening, WMS layers, georeferencing, vector ETL pipelines, spatial queries, and batch processing. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Software]]. Keywords: GDAL, OGR, raster processing, vector ETL, GeoTIFF, COG, geospatial CLI, command-line GIS
- [Customize a climate resilience index](https://learn.arcgis.com/en/projects/customize-a-climate-resilience-index/): Build a heat risk index with ArcGIS Pro's Calculate Composite Index tool, combining surface temperature, tree canopy, and population density to target tree-planting for extreme-heat mitigation. Related: [[Geospatial]], [[Remote_Sensing]]. Keywords: ArcGIS, composite index, heat risk, climate resilience, urban planning
- [ArcGIS Pro documentation — NDVI](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/arcpy/spatial-analyst/ndvi.html): The `arcpy.sa.NDVI` function computes the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index from red and NIR bands of a multiband raster. Related: [[Geospatial]], [[Remote_Sensing]]. Keywords: ArcGIS Pro, NDVI, vegetation index, arcpy, raster
