- [A national-scale sandy beach dataset for India derived from high-resolution satellite imagery and deep learning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-07408-8): A U-Net model trained on IRS ResourceSat-2 LISS-IV (5.8 m) imagery maps sandy beach extents along the entire Indian coastline, delivered in Shapefile format with an accompanying Google Earth Engine app. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Geospatial]].
