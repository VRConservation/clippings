- [Understanding the FIA Plot](https://brycefrank.com/blog/understanding-an-fia-plot/): Bryce Frank makes the US Forest Inventory & Analysis plot configuration spatial instead of aspatial, using Vermont FIADB as the worked example. Covers the clustered layout (four positions, 6.8 ft microplot / 24 ft subplot / optional 58.9 ft macroplot radii), why `PLOT` has one row per remeasurement, condition mapping (fragments partition a subplot, conditions roll up across them, with `SUBPCOND_PROP` → `CONDPROP_UNADJ`), and how `TPA_UNADJ` expansion factors turn tree records into per-acre biomass — with SQL for each step. Notes that fragment and tree geometries are never released publicly. Related: [[Forest]], [[Geospatial]], [[Software]]. Keywords: FIA, forest inventory, FIADB, plot design, condition mapping, expansion factors, SQL, biomass estimation, data model
- [How tall is that tree? What FIA's height code really tells you](https://pratyush-dh.github.io/projects/blog/): Pratyush Dhungana audits FIA tree heights in FIADB 9.5 and finds that the `HT` column silently mixes three kinds of value — `HTCD` 1 field-measured, 2/3 crew-estimated, 4 model-estimated. In each state's latest evaluation ~85% of live-tree records are field-measured, and 99.4% of the model-derived ones sit in Alaska, California, Oregon and Washington. Fitting 1,322 regional height–diameter models on measured trees only (RMSE ~10.3 ft), crews of broken-top trees land ~3% below the model on average yet beat it against the same tree's earlier measured height; a diameter-only model cannot audit crews (AUC 0.54). Swapping all non-measured heights for model heights shifts national aboveground biomass by only −0.12%, but the sign flips with a retransformation-bias correction. Argues for filtering on `HTCD` before fitting any height or growth model. Related: [[Forest]], [[Geospatial]]. Keywords: FIA, tree height, HTCD, height-diameter model, model bias, broken tops, biomass, NSVB, audit
- [Forest Stand Profile](https://pratyush-dh.github.io/projects/fia-forest-stand-profile/): Interactive US map of forest volume, live biomass, merchantable sawlog stock, growth, mortality and removals for every state and county, broken out by stand-size class (seedling/sapling → poletimber → sawtimber) as totals or per-acre density. Runs live queries against USDA's FIADB-API — the estimation engine behind the official EVALIDator tool — for each state's most current survey, so sampling error is carried through and nothing is simulated or interpolated; exports per-state and multi-state CSVs plus SVG maps. Related: [[Forest]], [[Geospatial]], [[Software]]. Keywords: FIA, FIADB-API, EVALIDator, stand profile, timber volume, sawtimber, biomass, county statistics, interactive map
- [State of Global Forest Carbon 2026](https://www.chloris.earth/reports/Chloris-2026-State-of-Global-Forest-Carbon.pdf): Chloris Geospatial report (Climate Week 2026) built on 26 years of satellite above-ground biomass from 2000, measuring how much forest carbon the tropics gained and lost and the drivers of biomass loss, with field case studies from Tree Aid, Acción Andina, Tropical Forest Alliance, and Earth Innovation Institute. Headline: 19 Gt net pantropical carbon loss since 2000, and degradation — forests thinning while still standing — accounts for the majority of it, a pathway area-based deforestation monitoring misses. Related: [[Forest]], [[Carbon]], [[Climate]]. Keywords: forest carbon, above-ground biomass, degradation, MRV, REDD+, Article 6, pantropical, carbon loss
- [To Protect Its Water, a Wyoming Town Is Investing in Forest Restoration](https://www.wri.org/insights/encampment-wyoming-forest-restoration-clean-water-fund): Encampment, WY, is the first Wyoming community to tap Clean Water State Revolving Fund financing for proactive forest restoration — a $1.2M loan (75% principal forgiveness, 0% interest) to thin ~600 acres in the Medicine Bow-Routt watershed, plus nature-based restoration and Good Neighbor Authority work. Forests supply >80% of US freshwater and Medicine Bow-Routt alone serves ~25% of Wyoming's population. Thinning costs ~$2,000/acre. Related: [[Forest]], [[Fire]], [[Confinance]]. Keywords: forest restoration, watershed, CWSRF, thinning, prescribed fire, drinking water, Wyoming
- [With support from loggers and (some) environmentalists, Congress unites on major wildfire bill](https://www.latimes.com/environment/story/2026-09-10/congress-unites-on-fix-our-forests-act): LA Times on the bipartisan Fix Our Forests Act, which would expedite forest thinning on public and tribal lands by cutting environmental review (now averaging 5–7 years), expanding permitting exemptions to 10,000 acres and limiting litigation windows — splitting environmental groups (Sierra Club and Center for Biological Diversity oppose; Nature Conservancy supports). Related: [[Forest]], [[Fire]], [[Confinance]]. Keywords: Fix Our Forests Act, forest management, thinning, permitting reform, timber, environmental review, prescribed fire
- [New Report: Family Forests Provide Millions in Benefits to Businesses and Communities](https://www.forestfoundation.org/why-we-do-it/family-forest-blog/new-report-family-forests-provide-millions-in-benefits-to-businesses-and-communities/): American Forest Foundation & Sorenson Impact Institute 'Beyond Carbon' report quantifies Family Forest Carbon Program co-benefits — $6.4M/yr healthcare savings from filtering air pollutants, 91% of properties having high importance to surface drinking water, $2.3M/yr regional economic output, and 54% higher financial outcomes for enrolled landowners. Related: [[Forest]], [[Confinance]], [[Climate]]. Keywords: carbon program, family forests, co-benefits, water quality, ecosystem services, healthcare savings, biodiversity
- [A global dataset of forest disturbance regimes derived from satellite biomass observations](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/18/5895/2026/): Novel framework derives four disturbance regime parameters (disturbance rate μ, gap-size α, severity β, background mortality Kb) from high-resolution satellite biomass data using an inverted ML model trained on 8M+ simulations. Produces first globally continuous, observationally constrained dataset at 25 km tile and 0.25° grid resolution with uncertainty layers. Related: [[Fire]], [[Geospatial]]. Keywords: disturbance regimes, aboveground biomass, GlobBiomass, ESA CCI, carbon cycle, Earth System Models, stochastic disturbance
