# Carbon

**Summary**: Measuring and managing terrestrial carbon — land-sector greenhouse gas emissions and removals, biomass change, and the data behind MRV.
**Last updated**: 09-28-2026.

---

- [Land GHG Monitoring System](https://landcarbonlab.org/data/land-ghg-monitoring-system/): WRI Land & Carbon Lab's open global system for tracking greenhouse gas emissions and removals across agriculture, soil, forests and other vegetation from 2016 at up to 30 m resolution. Bundles five datasets built on the same IPCC inventory guidelines countries use for national reporting — cropland management emissions (available, 2020, 10 km), livestock emissions (forthcoming), organic soil emissions (forthcoming, 30 m, five-year steps from 2000), mineral soil carbon gain and loss (available, 30 m, from 2000), and vegetation carbon fluxes (forthcoming, 30 m annual 2016–2024). Extends Global Nature Watch's forest flux method to all land; free and open for inventories, supply-chain accounting, and conservation planning.
