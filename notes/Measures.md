# Measures

**Summary**: How conservation effectiveness is measured and tested — funder practice for embedding action-level tests in grants, study design, and evidence gaps.
**Last updated**: 09-28-2026.

---

- [Role of funders in embedding tests in conservation practice](https://conbio.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/cobi.70309): Smith, Ockendon et al. in *Conservation Biology* — 25 representatives of 18 environmental funders, the Environmental Funders Network, and Conservation Evidence lay out 11 approaches for embedding well-designed tests of individual conservation actions into grant-making, ranging from encouraging tests in proposals, to mandating them, to ring-fencing separate test funds (1–3% of grant value), to backing an external testing unit for training and design advice; the right approach depends on grant size, type, and process. Also names 8 barriers — unfamiliarity with identifying informative tests, no capacity to check test quality, and the time needed to develop, run, and read out tests — with proposed solutions.
